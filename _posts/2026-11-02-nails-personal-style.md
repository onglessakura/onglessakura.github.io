People notice hands more than we think: when you shake hands, hold a coffee, sign a document or tuck your hair behind your ear. Well-kept nails are a quiet signal of care, much like a good haircut or a well-tailored coat. They're the finishing touch that pulls a whole look together.

## Find the look that feels like you

There's no single "right" manicure, only the one that suits your life and your personality.

- **Minimal and polished.** Short to medium length, soft shapes, and nude, milky or sheer pink tones. Ideal for the office, and timeless.
- **Classic and elegant.** Deep reds, soft burgundy or a refined French finish. Always appropriate, from a business meeting to an evening out.
- **Bold and creative.** Longer shapes, rich colours and nail art. A way to express yourself, and a great conversation starter.

## Shape, length and colour

A few principles help with every look:

1. **Shape follows your hands.** Rounded and oval shapes soften and lengthen the fingers. Squarer shapes feel modern and graphic.
2. **Length follows your lifestyle.** If you type, cook or train a lot, a shorter length stays beautiful longer.
3. **Colour follows the moment.** Lighter tones for a fresh, everyday feel. Darker, richer tones for evenings, autumn and winter.
