Head spa has become one of the most requested treatments in beauty salons — and once you've tried one, it's easy to see why. Here's what to expect.

## What exactly is a head spa?

A head spa is a scalp-focused ritual inspired by Japanese salon culture. Instead of treating only the lengths of your hair, it cares for the **skin of the scalp**: cleansing, exfoliating and massaging it, much like a facial does for your face.

## What happens during the treatment?

While every salon has its own ritual, a head spa typically includes:

1. **A short consultation** about your scalp and hair.
2. **Deep cleansing** to lift away product build-up and excess oil.
3. **A slow, rhythmic scalp and neck massage** — the part most clients look forward to.
4. **A nourishing treatment** for scalp and hair, followed by a gentle rinse.

The result: hair that feels lighter and cleaner, and a deep sense of calm.

## Who is it for?

Almost everyone. A head spa is especially enjoyable if you:

- spend long hours at a screen and carry tension in your neck and shoulders;
- use a lot of styling products or dry shampoo;
- simply want an hour of real quiet in the middle of a busy week.

If you have a scalp condition or recent skin irritation, mention it when you book so we can adapt the treatment.

## How often should you book?

Many clients enjoy a head spa **once a month** as a reset, or before a special occasion. It also pairs beautifully with a manicure or pedicure for a complete self-care afternoon.
