Not long ago, a man at the nail salon was usually there to wait for someone. That has changed. More and more men book manicures for themselves, some for clean, well-groomed hands and others for colour and nail art.

## A shift that started on stage and on the red carpet

Celebrities did a lot to make men's manicures mainstream. In 2020, *The Guardian* noted that nail art had become common among male stars such as Post Malone, A$AP Rocky, Pete Davidson and Bad Bunny, and that salons were seeing a more varied clientele as a result.

Then came the brands. In November 2021, Harry Styles, long known for his mismatched manicures, launched **Pleasing**, a gender-neutral beauty line that started with nail polish. As CNN reported at the time, he joined a growing list of male artists with their own nail products, including A$AP Rocky, Machine Gun Kelly and Lil Yachty. Tyler, the Creator has also added polish to his Golf le Fleur line.

## Why men are booking

- **Grooming, plain and simple.** Neat cuticles, even length and smooth edges make hands look cared for in a handshake, a meeting or a first date.
- **Self-care.** Half an hour of quiet, a warm soak and a hand massage are a genuine break from a busy week.
- **Self-expression.** For some, a black or navy polish, a single accent nail or minimal nail art is part of their personal style, like a watch or a ring.

Industry surveys point the same way. A 2023 Statista survey, reported by AOL, found that about a quarter of men aged 25 to 40 included manicures in their monthly grooming routine.

## What a classic men's manicure includes

If you've never had one, here is what to expect:

1. **Shaping:** nails trimmed and filed to a clean, even length.
2. **Cuticle care:** cuticles gently pushed back and tidied.
3. **Buffing:** a subtle natural shine, or a clear matte finish if you prefer.
4. **Hydration:** hand cream or cuticle oil to finish.

Colour is entirely optional. Many men keep it natural, while others choose a dark shade or a single accent nail.
